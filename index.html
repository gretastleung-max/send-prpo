Error Handling Framework

Level 1 - Fatal

  • Graph Authentication Failed
  • Master List Missing
  • Config Missing
  • Cannot Open Excel

Level 2 - Record Error

  • Missing Supplier Email
  • Invalid Template
  • Missing Attachment

Level 3 - Warning

  • Duplicate PO Email
  • PO Not Found
  • Extra Supplier Found

Level 4 - Information

  • PO Downloaded
  • PDF Generated
  • Email Sent
